The user-directed quality-hold revision replaces the original v2 shortage case.
V1 (`decision_demo.py`), its tests/configuration and the website are unchanged.
Three real Microsoft Agent Framework specialists and a Coordinator separate
**quality containment** from **delivery recovery**, using synthetic read-only
records. Both Decision-1 and GPT use existing **Azure Foundry deployments with
Entra authentication**. OpenAI-named packages are protocol adapters, not access
to the public OpenAI service.

Three additional synthetic cases now illustrate **expected 0 / 1 / 3 specialist
routes**, selected by the live models, not fixed presets. The quality case and
its `supply-chain` alias remain the advanced human-review example, unchanged.

## Role-specific analysis instructions

Each actor now receives its own decision process, followed by common typed-output,
provenance and untrusted-evidence rules. Coordinator synthesis duties are no longer
included in specialist instructions. A separate Quality addendum retains held-stock,
sample, notification and provisional human-window safeguards without imposing a
quality incident on healthy cases.

| Role | Required analysis and evidence boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Supply-chain | Compare each option's released stock/material allocations, every shipment leg, carrier confirmation/qualification, booking/pickup/transit/window and aggregate incremental freight. Expedited service, alternative carrier and approval authority are separate facts. Inventory alone cannot establish manufacturing capacity. |
| Production | Compare setup plus rounded-up run minutes against the actual cell-availability/staffing intersection, subtract reservations once, locate an unreserved slot and add packing separately. Check evidenced pickup times and actual other-order impact, not hypothetical overtime or unsupported optimality. |
| Commercial | Separate planning permission, preference, binding commitment and accepted dispatch. Compare customer benefit with cost/authority without inventing a monetary value for an unpriced preference or requesting freight approval solely because an authorized service is expedited. |
| Coordinator | Compare supported findings with canonical quantities/times/authority; explain a tradeoff without invented objective weights. Preserve all findings, unknowns, conflicts and same-option/unscoped requirements. Expose unsupported assertions rather than silently dropping them or presenting them as proven facts. |

For example, an available cell window ends at the earlier of `available_until`
and `staffed_shift_end`; packing within the staffed shift does not extend cell
availability. Freight approval follows documented authority, not the word
"expedited". An option's dependency flags describe that option, not every
alternative mentioned. Cross-domain evidence limits are stated explicitly;
owning a different tool is not itself missing business evidence.

These are model instructions, **not new deterministic guarantees**. Output
schemas, tools, host guards, routing thresholds and call/token caps are unchanged.
No role is given a preferred option, expected specialist count or private
chain-of-thought requirement. The existing concise-field guidance and1,200-token
output allowance remain.

## Two-stage routing

User-approved 15 October redesign: Decision-1 first decides **overall assessment
need**, independently of specialist relevance. Complete facts may still require
reasoning about a recovery option or its dependencies; missing data is not the
only reason to assess. A uniquely highest `no_further_assessment` at least70%
can finish directly, with **no expert proposal or proposal validation**.
Canonical missing evidence, Quality containment/notification or unsupported
freight authority override that shortcut and conservatively require assessment.

If assessment is needed, a second sequential Decision-1 request independently
scores the three roles. Confident `needed` consults; confident `not_needed`
skips. Weak/tied/`cannot_determine` scores conservatively consult the relevant
read-only specialist, **not a human routing question**. Uncertain overall need
also conservatively assesses. Raw scores are retained; model-reported confidence
is separate from the70%/unique-highest probability policy. The displayed
chosen-option probability is not the maximum of other options.

If overall assessment is needed but every role is confidently skipped, the
Coordinator assesses canonical records directly with empty finding references,
then undergoes normal proposal validation and policy review. It does not invent
specialist findings or call this a no-assessment result. Canonical records
supplied to any Coordinator enter its ledger; specialists still cite only their
executed allowlisted records. No new agent, tool or retry is introduced.

Additional-case routing includes derived arithmetic/check/applicability data:
no new production means no qualified-cell requirement, and an unchanged
customer window means no pull-forward request is required. Missing applicable
evidence remains missing; the summary does not assign roles or dictate verdicts.

### Decision framing, not missing-fact routing

Both overall assessment and role selection now use the same deterministic
canonical `routing_context`, separating:

- `decision_status`: the remaining objective, whether alternatives are still
  unselected, their IDs, and no selected answer.
- `observed_records`: evidence references, existing authority and documented
  planning permissions; the complete supporting records remain in `fixture`.
- `calculated_feasibility`: checked quantities, cell/readiness timing, every
  delivery leg and aggregate cost. Feasible does not mean already chosen.
- `unresolved_preferences`: for Pull-forward, the customer's stated full-receipt
  preference has no supplied monetary value; this is a decision tradeoff, not
  an invented mandatory approval or automatically missing business input.

Pull-forward explicitly asks to choose full-early versus split-standard. Both
share240 newly produced units and protected other-order reservations; one costs
1800 and avoids a second receipt, the other costs0 and uses two permitted windows.
Cost alone does not require escalation within the documented2500 limit.
Production retains a real cell comparison with fixed material/transport/customer
facts; the resolved carrier record has no outstanding alternative choice.
Quality's open business questions and immutable handoffs remain distinct.

Each role is asked **whether its expertise would contribute material analysis
to choosing between the alternatives or assessing the stated decision**.
Needed means material contribution even with complete evidence; not-needed means
no material contribution, not merely that records are complete. Domain
responsibilities are neutral, independent and nonexclusive. No expected count,
preferred option, new agent, summary call, threshold adjustment or forced route
is introduced. Routing framing does not repair contradictory GPT findings,
approvals or dependency flags; those remain independently reported and guarded.

The Coordinator's supplied canonical records are also
recorded in the evidence ledger. It can cite the policy/customer record even
when Commercial was legitimately skipped. This does not count as an extra
tool or model call; specialists still must read and cite their own allowlisted
tools, and cannot cite an unexecuted tool as their evidence.

Canonical cutoff/window, material/capacity/qualification, reserved-order,
carrier/authority and customer-reference checks independently require owner
review if unsupported—even if model validation is positive. Model-reported
unknowns, conflicts and approvals also prevent no-review completion. Typed
dependency flags preserve alternative-carrier/cell, pull-forward, overtime and
order-displacement requirements through synthesis, without keyword guessing.
No quality handoff is imposed where no quality incident exists.
This applies to scenario defaults: if a model nevertheless requests containment
or notification, it is explicitly labeled model-reported owner review, not
proof of a hold or suspect shipment in the fixtures. Operational freight
figures are incremental to standing contracted routes; $0 means no extra
service charge, not free transport.

Generic review supports pending or explicit leadership escalation with no
additional inference or contact. It cannot reuse the quality-only customer
rescheduling input to invent missing qualification, carrier or approval evidence.
The quality-hold recommendation-led menu below remains unchanged.

## One natural clarification

Human review starts with a recommendation to **contact the customer planner
about rescheduling the second 240 units**, keeping the first 180 at Friday
10:00 unchanged. The actual model findings and proposal remain visible.
If the Coordinator did not recommend a customer-window review, this is explicitly
labeled a **host-suggested review step**, not a model recommendation.
Required owner handoffs stay in the plan/final output, followed by the concise
reminder: "Planning update only; quality release, containment and required
approvals remain separate."

Choose one of two options; no JSON or pipe-separated input is needed:

```text
1. Record customer response after contact: customer will move the second delivery.
2. Reject the recommendation and request leadership escalation.
Choose 1 or 2: 1
Quantity customer reportedly agreed to move (1-240): 240
Revised delivery time (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM, UTC-05:00): 2026-10-17 08:00
Customer-planner response reference (1-200 characters): customer planner phone note
```

Choose 1 only to report a customer response received after contact, not merely
a willingness to reach out. This demo neither contacts the customer nor changes
an actual delivery date. Choice 2 (also `reject` or `escalate`) requests a
leadership handoff with no refresh or further model calls; it sends no message.
`reject`/`escalate` also stops during field collection.

The window uses the frozen snapshot's UTC-05:00 timezone. The reference is
required, nonempty and at most 200 characters; quantity must be a positive
integer no greater than the second delivery's 240 units; the revised time must
be later than its original window. Partial moves are supported. There is no
approve/release action.

The host creates a strict typed response through the real workflow
`request_info`/`response_handler` mechanism. Its source is **unverified human
clarification**. Reported agreement is not formal customer authority.
Production and Commercial refresh, Supply-chain reuses its finding, and
Coordinator/Decision-1 run once more. Unknowns can only disappear with relevant,
cited evidence; previous unresolved unknowns and conflicts cannot be dropped.

For the illustrative 240-unit move, the uncovered demand against the original
second window becomes provisionally 240 -> 0. **Inventory remains 180**, the
first delivery is unchanged, the later 240 still need replenishment, and
receipt/acceptance, formal customer review and shipped-lot containment remain
unresolved. Freight authority remains required if the proposal relies on freight,
even within a broader containment/recovery recommendation; a typed dependency
flag prevents the main action label from hiding that requirement.
Actual model recommendations/verdicts are not scripted.

EOF, interruption, non-TTY, invalid/empty input or expiry leaves review pending.
All prompts share the existing single 300-second human timeout and yield one
typed response through the same framework resume. No reprompt or automatic acceptance.

## Bounded calls and authority

Same three specialists, Coordinator, four tools and at most one continuation:
`read_inventory`, `read_shipment`, `read_build`, `read_approval_policy`.
Each specialist has at most 3 actual GPT attempts, 2 unique tools and 2 tool
rounds. Coordinator has one attempt and no tools. Existing reads can be batched
within a round; specialist dispatch remains sequential.

Hard ceilings are **20 GPT + 4 Decision-1 = 24 actual attempts** and 12 tool
executions. Failed requests and tool-result turns count. With one batched tool
round per specialist, an all-three initial assessment uses 7 GPT + 3 Decision-1;
refreshing Production/Commercial adds 5 GPT + 1 Decision-1. These are illustrative
normal paths, not promises about live output or latency.

No SDK retries, repair calls or hidden loops. Transport/tool guards enforce
limits even when the framework requests another final completion. This process
paces at 10 GPT / 20 Decision-1 attempts per rolling minute. Live deployment
inspection also confirmed a10,000-token/minute GPT limit. The transport now
paces a conservative local estimate (serialized UTF-8 request bytes/3 rounded
up, including schemas/tools, plus the full output allowance) within10,000 per
rolling minute. A single request above that demo ceiling fails before dispatch.
This is an estimate, not Azure's exact token accounting or a global reservation:
shared callers/dynamic service limits may still cause429, which is explicit and
stops without retry. Azure counts estimated prompt tokens and the full output
allowance, not just billed output ([quota documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/how-to/quota)).
Request 60s, credential 15s, tool 2s, active 900s including quota waits, human 300s,
wall 1200s. Human waiting pauses only the active clock. Worst-case calls can
take minutes; time can expire before count ceilings.

The 0.70 probability policy is illustrative, not calibrated confidence, safety
or compliance assurance. Exit 0 is advisory completion or explicit reject/
escalate, never real-world authorization; exit 1 is execution/contract failure;
exit 2 is pending/unresolved handoff. This case normally retains Quality-owned
handoffs even with positive proposal validation.
